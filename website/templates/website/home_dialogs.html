Farm help

Choose a question to get started.

How do I check my field?

Tap your field on the map, then press the yellow View results button. This saves the pin and opens crops, weather and your plan.

What does a crop score mean?

A score compares published crop requirements with available conditions for the selected area. Soil tests and local advice are still needed before planting.

Where is my crop rotation?

Open Crop, then choose View crop rotation. Edit the saved rotation in Field settings.

Do I need an account?

You can explore as a guest. Sign in to keep a separate field and calendar. Your profile button lets you view your account or sign out.
